The 2006 Football League Championship play-off final was an association football match played on 21 May 2006 at the Millennium Stadium in Cardiff , Wales, between Leeds United and Watford . The match was to determine the third and final team to gain promotion from the Football League Championship , the second tier of English football , to the FA Premiership . The winners of two semi-finals competed for the final Premiership place of the 2006–07 season . Winning the final was estimated to be worth up to £40 million to the successful team. The match was watched by a crowd of 64,736. It was the last play-off final to be held at the Millennium Stadium, as the new Wembley Stadium was completed in time for the 2007 final . Watford won the match 3–0 , with opening goalscorer Jay DeMerit named man of the match . Leeds goalkeeper Neil Sullivan scored an own goal to make the score 2–0 to Watford after 60 minutes, and the final goal was a penalty kick scored by Darius Henderson .
